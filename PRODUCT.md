# Grandline Services

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
People in Malawi browsing automotive shops/services or transport options, primarily on mobile. Help them compare supplied information and find the relevant category.

## Brand personality
Practical, clear, familiar. Auto Hub is the approved reference for marketplace navigation, imagery, typography and footer. Avoid sparse generic text-only shells and invented listings.

## Accessibility
Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion and responsive layouts.

## Release boundary
Frontend design first. Missing backend operations remain explicitly unavailable; no simulated enquiries, dispatch, fares or onboarding.
