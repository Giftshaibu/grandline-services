# Grandline marketplace design

Auto Hub is the visual source: Archivo headings, Inter body text, near-white surfaces, dark ink and red primary actions. Preserve the committed semantic tokens.

Use a full marketplace header with logo, product label, search, account controls and category dropdowns; mobile uses a native modal menu. Footer columns follow the current product and configured ecosystem destinations. Heroes combine illustrative editorial imagery with readable dark overlays, never fake inventory. Browsing uses category shortcuts, supplied-data cards and explicit unavailable states.

Keep 44px touch targets, visible keyboard focus, native dialog Escape/focus behavior, mobile layouts without horizontal overflow and reduced-motion support. Product information is primary; avoid decorative animation and fabricated trust badges.
